# If Opus 5.5 met Chinese aesthetics (interactive)

An original, from-scratch interactive/animated web piece inspired by the description of the RedNote video note **"假如opus5.5遇上中式美学"** by **Tristan** (小红书): https://www.xiaohongshu.com/discovery/item/6ae071250000000022036245

The film (made with Codex and a video model, referencing Anthropic's Opus 5.5 promo) moves from a horizon sunrise through flowing ink and growing cells to celadon glaze, with Chinese objects, crafts and architecture echoing each other in similar outlines, ending back at the opening Earth, a circle. No video frames, audio or other assets of the film were used or copied; everything here is procedurally drawn and written fresh, under the MIT licence.

Published as a GitBrew post.
